rewire-frontend-2.8: A Haskell-to-Verilog/VHDL compiler, front end (GHC driver, Eidos, Synolon)

The ReWire front end. The GHC driver and Core-to-Eidos bridge (ReWire.GHC.*), the Eidos typed functional IR and its passes (ReWire.Eidos.*), the Synolon machine-level IR, purification, and the Synolon-to-Hyle fold (ReWire.Synolon.*), and the pass-pipeline orchestration. Produces a ReWire.Hyle device; depends on rewire-backend for the Hyle IR and RTL backends and on rewire-base for shared utilities.
